SUPPORT CENTER

We're here to help.

Find answers to common questions below, or send us a message and we'll get back to you. This page is public — no PATH account or sign-in is required.

For interns

How do I create a PATH account?

Open the PATH app, choose "Create account," select "Student," and enter your email and password. You'll then complete your profile (personal details, education, and skills) before you can apply to internships.

How do I complete my profile?

After creating your account, PATH walks you through a short setup: personal details, your education, and the skills you'd like to be matched on. You can return and update any of this later from your Profile tab.

How do I upload or replace my CV?

From your Profile, choose to upload a CV (PDF only, up to 5 MB). Uploading a new file always replaces your current CV — PATH automatically re-scans it for skills so your profile stays up to date.

How do I add or edit my skills?

In your Profile's skills section, search PATH's skill list and tap a result to add it. If a skill you have isn't listed, type it in and choose "Add" to include it anyway — you're never limited to only what's already in the catalog.

How do I find internships?

The Internships tab shows opportunities from PATH-approved companies, which you can search and filter. You can also save internships to your Favorites to come back to later.

How do I apply for an internship?

Open an internship's details and tap Apply. You'll answer any questions the company has set up, and you can optionally authorize AI-assisted analysis of your CV for that specific application — see "How does PATH use AI in recruitment?" below.

How do I track my application status?

Your Applications tab lists every internship you've applied to, along with its current stage in the company's recruitment process.

How do I reset my password?

From the login screen, choose "Forgot password," enter your email, and enter the verification code we send you to set a new password.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account directly in the app (Profile → Delete Account), or use our secure web deletion page without installing the app. Either way, your account and associated data are permanently removed as described in our Privacy Policy.

How does PATH use AI in recruitment?

A company can optionally request AI-assisted analysis of a specific application's CV, using Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, to help judge whether it supports a particular internship requirement. This only happens for applications where you've explicitly given permission, which you can withdraw at any time. AI never automatically accepts, rejects, or advances an applicant — every hiring decision is made by the company. See our Privacy Policy for full details, including what's sent and how it's redacted.

For companies

How do I register a company?

Choose "Create account" in the app, select "Company," and enter your work email and password. You'll then complete your company profile before submitting it for review.

How does company verification work?

Every new company is reviewed by the PATH team before it can publish internships or appear to interns. This helps keep the platform free of fake or misleading listings. You'll see your review status in the app once you've submitted your company profile.

How do I publish an internship?

Once your company is approved, create a new internship listing with its details, required skills, and application deadline, then publish it so interns can discover and apply.

How do I review applications?

Each internship has an Applicants list showing everyone who applied, their profile, and their CV. You can open any application to see full details and move it through your recruitment pipeline.

How does skills-based matching work?

PATH's Smart ATS deterministically checks each applicant's CV against the skills you've marked as required, preferred, or bonus for the role, and shows you exactly where and how strongly each skill was found. This is rule-based matching, not AI, and never automatically rejects anyone.

How does AI-assisted screening work?

For an application where the applicant has explicitly authorized it, you can optionally request AI-assisted analysis (via Anthropic's Claude) of their CV against requirements your deterministic screening flagged as unclear. This is advisory only — it never changes an application's status, and it's refused outright if the applicant hasn't consented or has withdrawn consent.

How do I manage recruitment stages?

Each internship gets a recruitment pipeline (for example, Applied → Screening → Interview → Offer) that you can customize, and you can move applicants between stages as your process progresses.
