Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. Who we are

PATH is an internship discovery and application platform for candidates and organizations. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled when you use PATH’s mobile app or related company portal.

PATH is operated by [LEGAL OPERATOR NAME] at [BUSINESS ADDRESS]. For privacy questions or requests, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL].

2. Information you give us

Account information: your email address, password (stored as a one-way password hash), account role, and information needed to sign in or recover your account.

Candidate information: your name, date of birth, phone number, education institution, academic level and year, major, expected graduation year, career interests, selected skills, LinkedIn link, and any CV you upload.

Company information: organization name, industry, company size, location, website, description, representative name and contact information, and content of internship listings.

Application information: internships you apply to, submitted CV copies, cover letters, interest statements, links to LinkedIn or portfolios, responses to employer questions, and application status. We also process saved internships and other actions you choose to take in PATH.

3. Information created when you use PATH

PATH may extract text and identify matching skills from uploaded CVs to support discovery and relevance scoring. These automated skill matches can be incomplete or inaccurate.

We generate account and application identifiers, status records, and timestamps. The app and the services hosting PATH may also process technical information needed for authentication, service operation, error diagnosis, and security, such as device or network details, IP addresses, and server request logs. The exact logs depend on the production hosting configuration.

4. Why we use information

We use information to register and secure accounts, complete candidate and company profiles, publish and find internships, process applications, show application status, provide skill-based relevance scores, support users, review company access, investigate misuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Depending on the activity and applicable law, processing may be necessary to provide the service you request, pursue legitimate security and operational interests, comply with a legal duty, or act with your consent where consent is required.

5. When information is shared

When you submit an in-app application, the organization advertising that internship and its authorized recruitment users can access relevant application details, answers, and the CV copy submitted with that application. Replacing your current profile CV does not automatically replace the copy attached to an earlier application.

Company listings and related organization details may be visible to users browsing PATH. PATH administrators may access information necessary for support, security, and company review.

Hosting, database, file-storage, and email delivery providers may process information on PATH’s behalf to operate the service. We may disclose information if legally required or reasonably necessary to protect users or enforce applicable rights.

When you choose an external application link, the destination website handles any information you provide there under its own privacy practices. Employers receiving application information are responsible for their own subsequent handling of it.

6. CVs, matching, and decisions

PATH stores uploaded CV files and may locally extract text and skill indicators from them. Those indicators may influence internship matching or applicant-management displays, but a match percentage is informational and is not a decision that you are qualified or will be hired.

Employers make their own recruitment decisions. PATH does not guarantee placement and does not make a final hiring decision solely through its match score.

7. Storage, retention, and deletion

We retain account and profile information while it is needed to operate the account and for other legitimate purposes permitted by law. Application records and the CV copies associated with them may remain separate from a user’s current profile. Security records and backups may be kept for limited operational or legal reasons.

Retention depends on account activity, applications, legal obligations, security needs, and backup procedures. We aim to delete or de-identify information when it is no longer needed for these purposes, subject to legal requirements and applicable backup cycles.

You can request account closure, access, correction, or deletion at [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We will review and handle verified requests according to applicable law. Copies already received and independently retained by employers may require a separate request to those employers.

8. Your choices and rights

You can review or update profile information through the features PATH makes available. Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or obtain copies of personal information, or to object to certain uses.

Where a processing activity relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent as permitted by law. Contact [SUPPORT EMAIL] to make a privacy request or ask how PATH uses your information.

9. Security

PATH uses password hashing and account-based access controls. The mobile app stores authentication tokens using device secure storage. We take reasonable security steps appropriate to the service, but no online system is completely risk-free.

Please keep your login details private and report suspected unauthorized account access to [SUPPORT EMAIL].

10. Service providers and international processing

Information may be processed in countries other than where you live, depending on where PATH, its infrastructure providers, or recipient employers operate. Where required by law, we take appropriate steps to protect information during international transfers.

11. Eligibility

PATH is intended for people legally capable of creating an account under the Terms of Service. If you believe information about a person not eligible to use the platform has been collected, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL] so the concern can be reviewed.
